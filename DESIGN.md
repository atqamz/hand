# Design System: Hand board

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The On-Call Desk"**

The fleet is an on-call desk. Everything that needs the operator is an open item: a blocked screen, a failure, a decision or an unread report. Items are paged in one fixed severity order, acknowledged in place, and resolved into the timeline. The operator opens the board and knows at once whether anything is paged, because the masthead pill and the Needs count answer before the page settles. They acknowledge the top item where it sits (press "2 No", answer d5, mark r7 read), watch it collapse to its receipt while a Hand line lands in Chat, and then read s1's cards and steer it from the composer.

The material is a quiet review palette: a white ground, pale grey cards and panels on hairline rules and near-black ink, with a charcoal night theme whose cards sit one step above the ground. Accent blue marks the operator's own card, focus, links and the working pulse. Fail red is kept for trouble. Severity lives in pale header fills on bordered items, and every state carries a word and an icon shape as well as a colour. Type is the system sans at five sizes; mono is kept for refs, code, keys, terminal text, severity codes and live figures. The fleet tint touches only the mark and a 3px rule across the top.

The desk refuses the chat-ops shell of sidebar, chat and inspector, and it refuses the green-on-black terminal opposite. Every page shows one fleet. At 1024px and wider, Chat is a 760px main column of thread cards with the composer pinned beneath it, and Needs is a rail on the right. Below that width, the same two panels sit behind two tabs, Needs and Chat.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A white ground (`bg`) with pale grey cards (`card`) on hairlines (`line`) by day, and `#111317` with cards raised to `#1b1f25` at night.
- One severity ramp orders Needs, fills each item's header, and colours the Needs counts.
- Every state is a word plus an icon shape plus a colour, never colour alone.
- Chat is a thread of bordered cards: the supervisor on the left, the operator on the right on accent-bg, and Hand's own events as one-line ringed notes.
- Acknowledge in place: a pressed key collapses its item to "Sent 2 · waiting…", and every action leaves a receipt, a Hand line and a link back.
- A 44px masthead that answers the supervisor's state, model and context window.
- Five type sizes, tabular numerals for live figures, no web fonts, and a strict CSP.

## Colors

A cool neutral review palette with four signals and one accent: every saturated colour means one thing.

Every colour, radius and size is a custom property on `:root` in `internal/board/static/board.css`, the single source of the tokens; the frontmatter keys are the property names without `--`. Besides colours, `:root` holds the radii (`--r-sm` 4px, `--r-md` 6px, `--r-lg` 10px), `--control` (32px, 44px on coarse pointers) and `--badge` (20px). The night theme redefines the same properties inside `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark)`, and the `-dark` keys record those values. `:root` sets `color-scheme: light dark`, so native controls and scrollbars follow the system. There is no manual theme switch. Literal colours appear in three places only: the terminal excerpt's edge fade and scrollbar (`#6b7480`), the timeline's top fade mask, and the four favicons (see Components).

`TestTheOnCallPalette` pins twenty-two of these tokens to their values in both themes, and the radii, `--control` and `--badge`. `TestColoursMeetContrast` holds twenty-two text pairs to 4.5:1 in both themes (among them ink and muted on the ground, the card and the panel, accent on the ground, each signal on its pale fill and on the ground, ink on accent-bg, the terminal pair and the primary pair), the control edge to 3:1 on the ground, the card and the panel, and every fleet tint to 3:1 on the ground.

### Primary
- **Review Blue** (`accent`; pale pair `accent-bg`): the operator's own marks and the live signal. It fills the operator's chat cards (`accent-bg`) and colours their "you" label; it draws the focus ring, the caret, links and text selection (`accent-bg`). It is the working pill, the working pulse, the Chat tab's dot while the supervisor works, the context bar's fill below 80%, and the composer's frame while it has focus.

### Secondary
- **Ink** (`primary`, with `on-primary` text): the one committing button in a form: Send, Answer, Resume and Start. It is near-black with white text by day, and near-white with ground-coloured text at night, so it never competes with a signal.

### Tertiary
- **Fleet Tint** (`tint-0` to `tint-11`): twelve hues around the wheel at a similar mid lightness, each with a night value. `tint()` in `internal/board/board.go` picks one per fleet as FNV-1a 32 of the fleet id, mod 12, and the page sets `class="tint-N"` on `body`; a page with no fleet id gets `tint-0`. It colours the mark and the masthead's 3px top rule, and nothing else, with one exception the build carries: on the fleet list, a fleet's name turns its tint on hover.

### Neutral
- **Review Ground** (`bg`): the page, the masthead, the tabs, Needs items and chat cards at rest, inputs, keys, open menus and the toast.
- **Card Grey** (`card`): chat and thread card header strips, and code in replies.
- **Panel Grey** (`panel`): the composer box.
- **Ink** (`fg`): body copy, headlines, the current tab and its underline, the author in a card header.
- **Muted Ink** (`muted`, and `neutral` at the same value): meta, times, Hand lines, the hint, crumbs, placeholders, stopped states and quiet counts.
- **Hairline** (`line`): every rule and decorative border (card edges, dividers), the context bar's track, and scrollbar thumbs.
- **Control Edge** (`line-strong`): the outline of what the operator operates, where the border is the affordance: select, input, textarea, the composer box, default buttons and the paperclip. `#80889a` by day (3.56:1 on `bg`, 3.20:1 on `card`, 3.32:1 on `panel`) and `#6b7482` at night (3.94:1, 3.50:1 and 3.77:1), so it meets the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11; `TestColoursMeetContrast` pins both.
- **Hover Wash** (`hover`): the hover fill of buttons, menu rows and the model menu's summary.
- **Terminal** (`screen`, `screen-fg`): a blocked screen's excerpt, dark in both themes.
- **Shadow** (`shadow`): the colour of the one lift shadow, under open menus and the toast.

### Signals
- **Fail Red** (`fail`, pale `fail-bg`): blocked screens, failed attempts and an interrupted supervisor in Needs; the failing pill (blocked, unreachable, exited, interrupted, failed); failing task icons; blocked and failed report headers; the auto-deny countdown; the context bar and its figure from 90%; error toasts and error pages; and the two controls that end a turn, Interrupt, and Stop on hover or armed.
- **Wait Amber** (`wait`, pale `wait-bg`): decisions, limited attempts and "no supervisor" items in Needs; a pending model switch; the context bar and its figure from 80%; a stale-server note; waiting task icons.
- **Pass Green** (`pass`, pale `pass-bg`): the ready pill, running and passing task icons, done report headers, the All clear icon, and done states.
- **Neutral** (`neutral`, pale `neutral-bg`): reports and quiet attempts in Needs; the starting, stopped and no-supervisor pills; idle task icons; counts at rest.

### Named Rules
**The Red Is Trouble Rule.** Fail red marks only what blocks or has broken: a blocked screen, a failure, an interrupted or unreachable supervisor, a context window at 90%, the auto-deny countdown and an error, plus the two controls that end a turn. Decisions, reports, links and ordinary states never take it.

**The Word And Shape Rule.** A state is never colour alone. The masthead pill carries an icon and a word, each Needs header carries an icon and a severity code, and each task row carries its state icon.

**The Tint Stays Put Rule.** The fleet tint colours the mark and the masthead's 3px top rule. It never fills a surface or colours copy.

## Typography

**Body Font:** the system sans (`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", "Noto Sans", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`)
**Label/Mono Font:** the system mono, held in `--mono` (`ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", monospace`)

**Character:** one plain sans for every word the operator reads, and a mono that appears only where the text is data: refs, keys, terminal lines, severity codes and figures that tick. There is no display face and no web font.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 22px, 30px, -0.01em): thread titles on the task and decision pages, balanced and clamped to two lines.
- **Headline** (600, 18px, 24px, -0.01em): the Needs and Tasks heads and the fleet list's head.
- **Title** (600, 15px, 22px): the headline of an open Needs item, the fleet name in the masthead and `###` headings in replies. A collapsed item sets it at 400 on one line with an ellipsis.
- **Copy** (400, 15px, 24px): the body of chat and thread cards; chat cards are held to a 72ch measure and thread cards fill their column; tables and code blocks in replies may run wider.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 20px): the page default, task rows, crumbs and the composer's text.
- **Heading small** (600, 14px, 20px): `h3` heads on thread pages and `####` headings in replies; report titles in a report header use 14px at 600.
- **Nav** (600, 14px, 20px): the tabs, the model menu's summary and the model name in the composer.
- **Control** (400, 14px, 20px): buttons, with the primary at 600. Keys in a blocked screen take the same size in mono.
- **Badge** (600, 12px, 18px, tabular): every badge: the status pills, the Needs counts, the task groups' counts, deliverable links, report states and the countdown.
- **Label** (600, 12px, 18px): the task groups' heads and labels in menus.
- **Meta** (400, 12px, 18px, tabular): times, meta, card header strips, Hand lines, the working line, hints and notes.
- **Mono** (400, 12px, 18px, tabular): refs, the fleet id, `kbd` keycaps, the context figure and the countdown (at 600).
- **Severity code** (600, 12px, 18px, 0.03em, uppercase mono): the word in a Needs header (BLOCKED, FAILED, LIMITED, NO SUPERVISOR, INTERRUPTED, DECISION, REPORT, QUIET) and on the task page's waiting rows.
- **Code block** (400, 12px, 18px, mono): terminal excerpts and preformatted blocks.

### Named Rules
**The Five Sizes Rule.** Every pixel font size is 22, 18, 15, 14 or 12px. `TestTypeHasFiveSteps` fails on any other, with one pinned exception: inputs rise to 16px on coarse pointers so a phone never zooms. Inline code in replies sets at 12px.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Figures that update in place are set in tabular numerals so the row never shifts: the `.live` class (the context figure, the countdown), every `time`, counts, meta and refs.

## Layout

- **Fleet page below 1024px:** one column held to 760px and centred, with the masthead's inner row aligned to it. Two tabs switch between Needs (the open items, then Tasks) and Chat (the timeline, then the composer). The tab comes from the URL hash; with no hash it opens on Needs when anything waits and on Chat otherwise. The board never switches tabs for the operator. Without JavaScript both panels show and the tabs are anchors.
- **Fleet page at 1024px and wider:** a grid of Chat (`minmax(0,760px)`) and the Needs rail (`minmax(360px,400px)`) with a 32px gap, centred. The rail has a hairline on its left, 24px of padding inside it, and scrolls on its own; the tabs are hidden and the Needs head shows. The masthead's inner row aligns to the 1192px pair.
- **App shell (at least 600px tall):** the body fills `100dvh` and never scrolls. Needs and the timeline scroll inside themselves, and the composer's text box grows up to 40dvh.
- **Short screens (under 600px tall):** the page flows as one scroll. The masthead shrinks to 36px, sticks to the top and merges the tabs into its single row. The composer sticks to the bottom, except while the start picker is open, and its text box stops at 30dvh. Without JavaScript the page also flows, with the same sticky composer.
- **Phone (under 600px wide):** 12px board padding. The masthead stays one row: the mark, the fleet name (which truncates first), then the Needs count with its worst state (for example "1 blocked") and the status pill; the fleet id, the model, the queue, compactions and the context bar are hidden. The composer moves the hint to its own row above the controls, the Interrupt key drops its label, and the tabs split the width in halves. On a phone that is also short, the fleet name hides too.
- **Thread pages:** the task, decision, conversation, error and fleet-list pages use one 880px column with 20px, 16px and 48px of padding and a 14px gap, and the masthead's inner row aligns to its 848px content.
- **Chat:** the timeline stacks newest at the bottom (`column-reverse`), with a 12px gap, a 16px fade at its top edge and a stable scrollbar gutter on both sides. Without JavaScript it scrolls on its own up to 65vh; on short screens it flows with the page. When the operator is within 48px of the bottom, new entries and a growing composer keep it pinned.
- **Rhythm:** 4px and 8px steps inside components, 12px between chat entries and around card bodies, 20px between the sections of a panel, and a 24px board gap (32px between Chat and the rail). Rows in lists share one `.row`: 4px of padding, at least 40px high, between hairlines.

### Named Rules
**The Rail At Desk Width Rule.** Needs is a rail only from 1024px (`TestNeedsIsARailAtDeskWidth`); below it the tabs stay (`TestTabsBelowDeskWidth`) and each panel is held to the 760px column.

**The Five Regions Rule.** The fleet page updates live through exactly five regions, `status`, `console`, `timeline`, `queue` and `tasks`, and the composer form sits outside every region so a live update never takes the operator's draft.

**The Things In New Tabs Rule.** From the Board, things open in a new tab and places navigate in place; outside links always open in a new tab. A thing is a task, attempt, report, decision, deliverable, image or outside page; a place is a fleet, the Board, Tasks, Conversation, a crumb, a pager step or an anchor. The fleet page's task rows, Needs links and refs in replies and Hand lines carry `target="_blank" rel="noopener"` (`TestAwayOpensLiveRefsInANewTab`); the Tasks page, the conversation page and the thread pages browse refs in place (`TestTheTasksPageBrowsesInPlace`, `TestTheLogAndThreadPagesKeepRefsInPlace`). Middle-click and Ctrl-click work everywhere.

## Elevation & Depth

The desk is flat. Depth comes from tone and hairlines: cards and header strips on `card` or a pale signal fill, borders in `line`, and a night card one step above the night ground. Only two things float, the open menus and the toast, and they share one soft lift. The keys of a blocked screen carry a 1px keycap edge, and `kbd` keycaps a 2px bottom border instead of a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px var(--shadow)`): the open model menu, the overflow menu and the toast.
- **Focus frame** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px var(--accent)`): with an accent border, the composer box's 2px frame while its text box has focus. A focus state, not depth.
- **Keycap edge** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 var(--line)`): the bottom edge of each key under a blocked screen.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Desk Rule.** Resting surfaces carry a hairline and no shadow. `TestTheOnCallPalette` fails on any `box-shadow` outside the composer box, the menus, the toast, the keys and `kbd`.

## Shapes

- **Corners:**
  - 10px (`rounded.lg`) on bordered items and floating boxes: Needs items, chat cards, thread cards, the composer, menus, the toast and a decision's option buttons.
  - 6px (`rounded.md`) on controls and boxes: buttons, keys, inputs, the terminal excerpt, code blocks and tables in replies, thumbnails and the skip link.
  - 4px (`rounded.sm`) on the focus ring, `kbd` keycaps, inline code and the overflow menu's rows.
  - Pills (999px) on the badge, the context bar and the scrollbar thumbs.
  - Circles on the Hand line's ring (8px) and the Chat tab's dot (6px).
- **Lines:**
  - Borders and rules are 1px hairlines. A list of rows (tasks, attempts, events, decisions, fleets) has a hairline above its first row and after each row.
  - A bordered item's header strip ends on a hairline.
  - In Chat, day dividers are the only full-width rules.
  - A quote in a reply carries a 1px hairline on its left, not a coloured stripe.
  - The masthead has a 3px rule in the fleet tint on top and a hairline below.

### Icons
Icons are Hand's own inline SVG, kept in `internal/board/templates/icons.html`.
- **Construction:** each uses a 16 by 16 viewBox and `aria-hidden="true"`, with one to four paths. Line icons stroke in `currentColor` at 1.5 with round caps and joins; solid icons fill in `currentColor`. `TestIconsAreOwnInlineSVG` checks all of this and rejects Octicons.
- **Size and colour:** 16px, except the mark at 22px and the pill's icon at 12px. They take their colour from the surrounding state.
- **The state family:** failure is a circle with an X, passing a circle with a tick, waiting a circle with three dots, running an open arc around a solid dot, and idle a dashed circle.
- **The rest:** the mark is a dial with a needle. The chevron beside the model menu turns 180° when the menu opens. Interrupt is a solid rounded square and the overflow icon is three solid dots. A bell is defined in the set, but no page draws it since Notify moved into the overflow menu.

## Components

### Masthead
A 44px row on the ground with the fleet tint's 3px rule on top and a hairline below, on every page.
- **Left:** the mark in the fleet tint and the fleet name (Headline), linking home, then the fleet id as a borderless muted ref.
- **Right, in order:** on phone only, the Needs count; the status pill; the supervisor and its model ("s1 · opus high", muted); "2 queued" when above zero; the context bar; "compacted 2×" when above zero. A stale-server note follows in amber, on its own line where the strip wraps (phone and short screens).
- **Link state:** when live updates drop, a muted note reads "Reconnecting…", then "Board offline since 12:41", or "Log in again: hand open --print" when the board refuses the session.
- **Thread pages** carry the same masthead with a static copy of the strip.

### Status pill
A badge, 24px high (`--badge` plus 4px) with a 12px icon and a lower-case word.
- **Working:** accent on accent-bg with the running icon, which pulses to 35% opacity and back on a 2s ease-in-out loop.
- **Ready:** pass on pass-bg with the passing icon.
- **Failing:** fail on fail-bg with the failure icon, worded blocked, unreachable, exited, interrupted or failed. An ended supervisor's reason shows as its tooltip.
- **Neutral:** muted on neutral-bg with the idle icon, worded starting, stopped or no supervisor.

### Context bar
A native `meter`, 48 by 6px on a hairline track, followed by the figure in tabular mono ("510K / 1M"). The fill is accent below 80% and amber from 80%, where the figure turns amber too; from 90% both turn red, the figure at 600 with "· compact soon" added. The figure alone shows when the window is unknown, and nothing shows before there is context data.

### Tabs
Below 1024px, a bar under the masthead row with a hairline between.
- **Labels:** "Needs" with its count and "Chat", in Nav type and muted ink, 10px by 12px (10px by 14px and content width from 600px wide), turning to ink on hover.
- **Current tab:** ink text and a 2px ink underline.
- **Needs count:** a neutral badge that takes the worst open item's tone, red or amber.
- **Chat:** a 6px accent dot while the supervisor works.

### Needs items
The signature component. Each open item is a `details` element with a 1px hairline border and 10px corners on the ground, 8px apart, most severe first; the top item is open and the rest are collapsed. Up to 50 show, then a line counts the rest. A muted chevron at the right of every summary turns 180° when the item opens, so a collapsed item reads as something to open. The keyboard focus ring sits inside the item (`outline-offset:-2px`), because the item clips anything drawn outside it. In the rail, `#needs` keeps its scrollbar gutter so the width never jumps, and the Needs head sticks to the top of the rail.

| a blocked supervisor screen | 1 | fail | BLOCKED |

| a failed attempt | 3 | fail | FAILED |
| a supervisor or an attempt stopped at a usage limit | 4 | wait | LIMITED |
| work waiting with no supervisor | 5 | wait | NO SUPERVISOR |
| an interrupted supervisor | 6 | fail | INTERRUPTED |

- **Header:** a grid of a 16px state icon, a slug line and a right-hand cell, over the headline on its own row, padded 8px by 12px on the tone's pale fill (`fail-bg`, `wait-bg` or `neutral-bg`). The icon takes the tone: failure for fail, waiting for wait, idle for neutral.
- **Slug line:** the severity code in the tone's colour, then the refs, harness and model, status and time, separated by an 8px gap. Refs in the slug take the line's size and colour, and linked ones underline in hairline colour. On a red or amber header the slug text mixes 55% of the tone into the ink.
- **Headline:** Title type; hovering the header underlines it in hairline colour.
- **Body:** a grid with a 10px gap, padded 10px by 12px by 12px, holding the item's action:
  - a blocked supervisor screen shows its terminal excerpt and its keys, then "Type queued messages anyway" with a hint for a screen Luvus misread;
  - a blocked worker screen names the attempt and its task, then shows its excerpt and keys;
  - a decision shows its question (up to 40vh, then it scrolls), option buttons that fill the answer box, the answer box, the primary Answer and an "Open dN" link;
  - a failure shows its reason in muted ink and a "View aN" link;
  - a report shows its excerpt, Mark read and an open link;
  - a quiet attempt says it stopped without a report and links to it;
  - a limited attempt's headline is its limit line, and its body says it stopped at a usage limit and links to it;
  - a limited supervisor's headline is its limit line, and its body says Hand holds wakes until that line leaves the screen;
  - a no-supervisor item shows Resume or Start, and an interrupted supervisor shows the primary Resume and New session.
- **Countdown:** Claude Code's auto-deny countdown sits in the header's right-hand cell as a live timer ("0:42", then "denying…") in red tabular mono on a ground badge.
- **Opening and closing** animate the body's height over .24s where the browser supports `interpolate-size`.
- **Arrival:** a new item's header starts 35% brighter and settles over 1.6s.

### Acknowledge in place
The signature interaction. Pressing a key on a blocked screen marks the item sent: the header's right-hand cell reads "Sent 2 · waiting…" in muted meta, the countdown hides, the keys dim to 55% and stop taking input, and the item collapses. A receipt toast ("Sent 2 to s1") shows for 4s. The next live update then removes the item and a Hand line lands in Chat. Answers and Mark read send the same way through `data-fetch` and leave their own receipt ("r7 marked read"); their item leaves Needs on the next update rather than collapsing to a sent state first.

### Blocked screen and keys
- **Terminal excerpt:** the screen's last lines in Code block type on the terminal colours with 6px corners, padded 10px top and bottom and 12px on the left. In a Needs item the lines wrap; a right-edge fade and a thin scrollbar remain for anything wider.
- **Keys:** a wrapping row with 6px gaps. Each key is a default button in mono, at least 2.6em wide, with the keycap edge, labelled from the screen's own options ("1 Yes", "2 No") or the fixed key set. Each key is its own form carrying the screen revision and digest, so a stale screen refuses the press.

### All clear
With nothing open, Needs shows the passing icon in green, "All clear" in ink at 600, and in muted ink the time it was last cleared and a summary: "All clear since 12:41 · 1 running · 6 inbox".

### Tasks
Under a "Tasks" head, grouped by status (Active and Inbox, then a Finished group holding the five most recent finished tasks), each group with a muted label head and a count badge. The Finished count is every finished task, not the five shown.
- **Row:** a `.row` of the state icon, the task ref, the title (one line, ellipsis), and a muted meta line ("a9 claude opus · blocked") that ends with the deliverable badge, at least 48px high between hairlines.
- **Icon colour:** failing red, waiting amber, running and passing green, idle neutral.
- A "Browse finished tasks (N)" link, 12px, sits under the list and opens the finished-tasks page. With `?all=1` the link reads "Hide finished tasks" and every task lists.

### Chat cards
- **Supervisor card:** on the left, fit to its content between 16em and 90% of the column, with a hairline border and 10px corners on the ground. Its header strip, on `card` with a hairline under it, holds "s1" in ink at 600, the model when it changed, and the time, all in Meta type. The body is the reply's markdown in Copy type, padded 10px by 12px.
- **Operator card:** on the right, up to 75% of the column, filled and bordered in accent-bg with a transparent header strip. "you" is in accent, then the time and, on the right, the delivery state: "queued", "delivered 07:18" or "typed anyway 08:17". While the supervisor reads as blocked, a queued card offers "Type anyway". The text keeps its line breaks. Each `[image: PATH]` line that points into this fleet's `inbox/` shows as a thumbnail under the text instead. A thumbnail is at most 240 by 180 px, with the hairline border and the 6px radius, and it opens the full image.
- **Feed:** an entry new to the timeline rises 1.4em and fades in over .28s.

### Hand lines
Hand's own events take one muted 12px line each, with no card: an 8px ring outlined in muted ink, the text clamped to one line with refs inline, and the time on the right. Consecutive wakes fold into one line, "3 wakes · 12:28–12:29", that expands to the individual lines indented 16px. Day dividers are centred muted meta between two hairlines. Cards under a divider show the clock time only; the divider and the times use the browser's zone, and without JavaScript both use UTC and the divider says so. While the supervisor works, a working line reads "s1 working · 4m · last output 12s ago · on your 07:19 message", with the message part linking to that card.

### Composer
One box on `panel` with a hairline border and 10px corners, padded 10px, pinned under the timeline. Its focus frame is an accent border plus a 1px accent ring.
- **Draft:** the text survives in-place navigation. It is kept in `sessionStorage` per tab and fleet, restored on load when the box is empty, and cleared by a successful send; with storage blocked the box works without it (`TestTheDraftSurvivesInPlaceNavigation`).
- **Text box:** borderless and transparent, growing with its text up to 40dvh (30dvh on short screens and without JavaScript).
- **Foot row:** the console region on the left, the hint in muted meta, then the send group. When the chat column is under 700px wide (the rail beside it at 1024px, or a phone) the hint takes its own row above the console and Send.
  - **Send group:** the paperclip "Attach images" button (a square of `--control`), the send key hint ("Ctrl Enter", or "⌘ Enter" on Apple devices, hidden on coarse pointers) and the primary Send.
  - **Hint:** while the supervisor is blocked, it links to Needs. While images upload, it reads "Attaching n image(s)…". With no supervisor running, the hint and the send group share one row above the Start strip, and Send turns from primary to a default button while a Start or Resume form is in the console.
  - **Attaching:** paste, a drop on the composer and the paperclip each attach png, jpeg, webp or gif images up to 10 MiB. During a drag, the composer shows an accent dashed outline.
- **Console region:** the model menu (its summary in Nav type with the chevron); a pending switch in amber with Cancel; Interrupt while a turn runs, a key of `--control` height with a red border and text, the stop-square icon and its label; Stop, a muted key that turns red on hover and asks "Press again to stop" on a red pale fill for 4s; and the overflow menu. With no supervisor running, a hairline then the Start strip take the foot row's place: one row with the profile select (`default` preselected, and required when profiles exist) and the single primary Start, then the closed "Other harness, model or effort" details, whose claude, codex, opencode and agy rows align as model, effort and Start. On a phone the selects come first and the button spans the row. With no routing policy the details start open. After a stopped supervisor, Resume is the primary, the select reads "same as sN · model effort" and its button says "Start new". The empty thread is a centred note above the composer, not a void.

### Menus
The model menu and the overflow menu are `details` elements whose body opens above the summary on the ground, with a hairline border, 10px corners, the lift shadow and 12px padding. The model menu offers a profile select (each profile naming what it runs), model and effort lists for the current harness, and "Switch now" or "Switch after this turn", then one switch form per other harness. The overflow menu is a column of `--control`-high rows with 12px side padding, 4px corners and the hover wash: Notify me, New session and Other harness. Escape and a click outside close any open menu.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded corners (6px).
- **Height:** every button, input, select, the paperclip and the model menu's summary is `--control` high (32px; 44px on coarse pointers), and a key is at least that wide. The text of a button without an icon is trimmed to its cap height (`text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic`) and the box padded by `(--control - 1cap) / 2`, so capitals sit on the box's centre whatever the font; browsers without `text-box` fall back to centring the line.
- **Default:** ground fill, control-edge border, ink text in Control type, padded 0 by 12px; hover takes the hover wash.
- **Primary:** Ink with `on-primary` text at 600 and no visible border, one per form; hover mixes 14% of the ground into it.
- **Disabled and busy:** disabled buttons fade to 55%; a form in flight fades its buttons to 60% with a progress cursor.
- **Focus:** a 2px accent outline, 2px out, with 4px corners, on every focusable element.

### Refs and badges
- **Ref:** plain mono text at 12px in the surrounding colour; a linked ref is accent and underlines on hover. Inside slug lines and Hand lines a ref takes the line's colour.
- **Badge:** the one `.badge`, a pill at least `--badge` (20px) wide and high, in Badge type, with a transparent 1px border and 0 by 8px padding. The text is trimmed to its cap height and the box padded by `(height - 1cap) / 2 - 1px`, so capitals sit within 0.6px of the centre. The status pill and the masthead's Needs count are `--badge` plus 4px (24px), set through `--size` on the badge. Its tone comes from one set of rules keyed on the attribute the markup already carries, and a badge with none is neutral: `data-tone` (`fail`, `wait`, `pass`, `link`), `data-state` (the pills: `failing` red, `waiting` amber, `ready`, `running` and `passing` green, `working` accent), `data-status` (a report's `stuck`, `progress` and `done`) and `data-worst` (a Needs count: red for blocked, worker, failure and resume; amber for decision, nosup and limited). The badges are the status pills, the Needs counts (masthead, tab and head), the task groups' counts, the severity word on the task page's waiting rows and the error page, the auto-deny countdown, a report's state and the deliverable link (`PR`, `MR` or `link`, outlined in a hairline, opening in a new tab). In a Needs header the severity word drops its fill and padding.
- **Corner mark:** an outside link (a deliverable chip, or a link in a reply, plan, report or question) ends in a 5px corner drawn with two 1.5px borders, no glyph and no image, at 70% of the text colour (`TestOutsideLinksCarryACornerMark`). A ref carries no mark, since every ref on the Board opens a tab.
- **Deliverable file:** plain muted mono text with no box (its basename cut with an ellipsis, the full path in its title).
- **Option:** the buttons that fill a decision's answer: `--control` high at least, 10px corners, left-aligned and wrapping.
- **Keycap:** `kbd` is `--badge` high with a 2px bottom edge.
- **Forced colours:** every badge draws a `CanvasText` border, so a tone is never lost with the fill.

### Rows
One `.row` serves the attempt and task rows, the waiting rows and decision rows on the task page, and the fleet list. It is a flex line at least 40px high with 4px of padding and a hairline under it, with five named slots in this order:
- **lead:** the state icon or the fleet's mark (`.icon`, `.mark`);
- **ref:** the ref or the slug (`.ref`, `.slug`);
- **body:** the title (`.check-title`, `.check-body`, `.need-title`, `.fleet-name`), which takes the free width and truncates;
- **meta:** the muted meta text (`.meta`), shrinking before the body;
- **tail:** everything else, in markup order: a time, a form, a link or an answer.

A slot that is missing leaves no gap. `.wrap` lets the waiting and decision rows wrap their body onto its own line on a narrow screen, and a decision's answer takes a line of its own. Task rows keep their 48px height and the whole row as the link; fleet rows are 48px.

### Inputs
Inputs and selects are `--control` high on the ground with a control-edge border, 6px corners and 0 by 10px padding; text boxes pad 8px by 10px. Focus draws a 2px accent outline inset by 1px and hides the border. Placeholders are muted.

### Reply markdown
Supervisor replies render a safe subset: paragraphs and lists 8px apart, where a numbered list keeps the numbers written in the source (a list a block interrupts resumes at its own number), lines indented under an item nest inside it up to six lists deep (deeper lines render as plain paragraph text), and blank lines between items keep one list; a numbered list starts at the number written when it fits 32 bits; `###` in Title and `####` in Heading small; inline code on `card` with 4px corners; code blocks on `card` with 6px corners that scroll rather than wrap; a hairline rule; quotes with a 1px hairline on the left in muted ink; struck text in muted ink. Tables fit their content inside a bordered 6px box that scrolls sideways, with a `card` header row in muted 12px at 600, cells at least 10ch wide padded 6px by 10px, tabular numerals, alignment from the source, and a sticky first column divided by a hairline.

### Thread pages
- **Head:** crumbs in muted 14px with refs, the title in Display type, a slug line with the status badge, refs, time and deliverable badges, then one hairline rule.
- **Task page:** waiting rows for its open decisions and unread reports, each with a filled severity code; the goal and plan as cards with Copy filling the card; attempts as task rows; reports as bordered cards whose header strip is neutral-bg, fail-bg when blocked or failed, or pass-bg when done, holding the ref, the summary, "from aN", the state word, the time and Mark read or "read by"; decisions with their answers; events as a grid of time, kind in plain words and detail.
- **Finished-tasks page** (`/<fleet>/tasks`): the head, then a GET form on a grid of 150px-minimum columns: Search across the full width, then Status, Project (only when the fleet has several), Since and Until as native date inputs (labelled UTC) and the primary Search button with a Clear link while a filter is set. Inputs and selects are 32px high. A muted 12px count line ("46 tasks") sits above the results. Results are task rows in the Tasks region's markup, grouped by day under group heads with count badges ("Tue 6 Oct UTC 5"), each row carrying the finish time in muted tabular meta before its PR badge; the server groups in UTC and JavaScript regroups in the browser's zone, as the timeline does, dropping "UTC". An empty result reads "No tasks match." in muted ink. Previous and Next links sit under the list at the left and right edges, keep every filter, and grow to 44px on touch. The page works without JavaScript, claims none of the five fleet-page regions and carries no composer.
- **Decision page:** the headline, the question as markdown (the whole question shows; only Needs caps it at 40vh), option buttons, the answer box and the primary Answer. Once answered, the answer shows as an operator card.
- **Error page:** one card with the status code as a red severity code, the message and a link back. It never shows a path.
- **Fleet list:** one row per fleet with its mark in its tint, its name and its id.

### Toast and receipts
A fixed box 16px from the bottom right, up to 28em wide, on the ground with 10px corners, the lift shadow and 10px by 14px padding, announced politely to screen readers. Errors take a red border and stay 8s; receipts take a hairline border and stay 4s.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from the custom properties in `board.css` (`var(--fail)`, `var(--accent-bg)`), and give any new token both a light and a dark value that keeps 4.5:1 for text.
- **Do** order anything that needs the operator by the severity ramp in `internal/board/queue.go`, and fill its header with its tone's pale fill.
- **Do** give every state a word and an icon shape as well as its colour.
- **Do** keep font sizes to 22, 18, 15, 14 and 12px, and set figures that update in place in tabular numerals.
- **Do** leave a trace for every action: a receipt, a Hand line in Chat, and a link back to the item.
- **Do** keep one primary button per form, and keep Stop behind a second press.
- **Do** keep all style in `board.css` and all script in `app.js`: no inline script, no inline style and no `style` attribute.
- **Do** stop every animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`: the working pulse, the feed, the arrival wash and the open and close transition.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use fail red for decisions, reports, links or ordinary states.
- **Don't** put a shadow on a resting surface; only the open menus and the toast lift.
- **Don't** colour copy or fill a surface with the fleet tint.
- **Don't** bring back the newsroom words (WIRE, FLASH, BULLETIN, URGENT, ROUTINE, SERVICE, dispatch, news budget, tray) or the canary, carbon, ink band, `h-*` and `pencil` tokens.
- **Don't** load web fonts or images from the stylesheet.
- **Don't** draw an icon as a text glyph or pull in an icon library; add it to `icons.html` in the 16px, 1.5-stroke family.
- **Don't** show the Needs rail below 1024px, and don't switch tabs for the operator.
- **Don't** build a chat-ops shell of sidebar, chat and inspector, or a green-on-black terminal look beyond the screen excerpt.
