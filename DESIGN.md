# Design System: Hand board

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The On-Call Board"**

The fleet is run from an on-call board. Everything that needs the operator is an open item: a blocked screen, a failure, a decision or an unread report. Items are paged in one fixed severity order and grouped under one head per severity word, acknowledged in place, and resolved into the conversation. The operator opens the board and knows at once whether anything is paged, because the masthead pill and the Needs count answer before the page settles. They acknowledge the top item where it sits (press "2 No", pick an option on d5 and send it, mark r7 read), watch it collapse to its receipt while a Hand line lands in the conversation, and then read s1's reply and steer it from the composer.

The material is a quiet review palette: a white ground, pale grey cards and panels on hairline rules and near-black ink, with a charcoal night theme whose cards sit one step above the ground. Accent blue marks the operator's own bubble, focus, links, the picked option and the working pulse. Fail red is kept for trouble. Severity lives in pale header fills on bordered items, and every state carries a word and an icon shape as well as a colour. Type is the system sans at five sizes; mono is kept for refs, code, keys, terminal text, severity codes and live figures. The fleet tint touches only the mark and a 3px rule across the top.

The Board is built for one operator who works beside an editor or on a phone. Navigation is one slim rail of icons at the left edge, 56px wide, that names its items on hover, on focus or on request; below 900px the same list opens from the masthead as a sheet. At 900px and wider the fleet page is that rail and two panes. The work pane is the widest thing on the page and holds Needs in severity bands, then Tasks with its search field. The supervisor pane on the right holds the supervisor's controls, its conversation, set as replies on the pane and the operator's bubbles, and a slim composer. A separator between the panes is dragged to size the chat: one remembered width for the whole board, never under 360px for the chat or 440px for the work pane, and no resizing below 900px, where three tabs (Needs, Tasks and Chat) show one panel at a time. A decision is answered from a choice list in Needs or on its page. From the Board, things (a task, a report, a decision, a deliverable) open in a new tab and places (the rail, a crumb, a pager step) navigate in place, so the Board stays where the operator left it. Every page shows one fleet; the Tasks page searches all of its tasks. The Board refuses a text sidebar with a conversation and an inspector beside it, and it refuses the green-on-black terminal opposite.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A white ground (`bg`) with pale grey cards (`card`) on hairlines (`line`) by day, and `#111317` with cards raised to `#1b1f25` at night.
- A 56px icon rail and two panes from 900px, three tabs and a sheet below it: the work pane stays the widest thing on the page, and the chat is dragged to a remembered width inside fixed limits.
- One severity ramp orders Needs into bands, fills the open item's header, and colours the band heads and the Needs counts.
- Every state is a word plus an icon shape plus a colour, never colour alone.
- The conversation is not a stack of cards: the supervisor's replies are bordered `card` bubbles on the left with their head line inside, the operator's messages are accent-bg bubbles on the right, and Hand's own events are one-line ringed notes.
- A decision is a choice list: numbered options with their consequences, one marked recommended, and a note.
- Acknowledge in place: a pressed key collapses its item to "Sent 2 · waiting…", and every action leaves a receipt, a Hand line and a link back.
- Things open in a new tab and places in place; the composer's draft survives either.
- A 44px masthead that answers the supervisor's state, model and context window.
- Five type sizes, tabular numerals for live figures, no web fonts, and a strict CSP.

## Colors

A cool neutral review palette with four signals and one accent: every saturated colour means one thing.

Every colour, radius and size is a custom property on `:root` in `internal/board/static/board.css`, the single source of the tokens; the frontmatter keys are the property names without `--`. Besides colours, `:root` holds the radii (`--r-sm` 4px, `--r-md` 6px, `--r-lg` 10px), `--control` (32px, 44px on coarse pointers) and `--badge` (20px). The night theme redefines the same properties inside `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark)`, and the `-dark` keys record those values. `:root` sets `color-scheme: light dark`, so native controls and scrollbars follow the system. There is no manual theme switch. Literal colours appear in three places only: the terminal excerpt's edge fade and scrollbar (`#6b7480`), the timeline's top fade mask, and the four favicons (see Components).

`TestTheOnCallPalette` pins twenty-two of these tokens to their values in both themes, and the radii, `--control` and `--badge`. `TestColoursMeetContrast` holds twenty-two text pairs to 4.5:1 in both themes (among them ink and muted on the ground, the card and the panel, accent on the ground, each signal on its pale fill and on the ground, ink on accent-bg, the terminal pair and the primary pair), the control edge to 3:1 on the ground, the card and the panel, and every fleet tint to 3:1 on the ground.

### Primary
- **Review Blue** (`accent`; pale pair `accent-bg`): the operator's own marks and the live signal. It fills the operator's chat bubbles (`accent-bg`) and colours their "you" label; it draws the focus ring, the caret, links and text selection (`accent-bg`). It is the working pill, the working pulse, the dot on the Chat tab and the rail's Conversation item while the supervisor works, the picked row of a choice list, the context bar's fill below 80%, and the composer's frame while it has focus.

### Secondary
- **Ink** (`primary`, with `on-primary` text): the one committing button in a form: Send, Answer, Resume and Start. It is near-black with white text by day, and near-white with ground-coloured text at night, so it never competes with a signal.

### Tertiary
- **Fleet Tint** (`tint-0` to `tint-11`): twelve hues around the wheel at a similar mid lightness, each with a night value. `tint()` in `internal/board/board.go` picks one per fleet as FNV-1a 32 of the fleet id, mod 12, and the page sets `class="tint-N"` on `body`; a page with no fleet id gets `tint-0`. It colours the mark and the masthead's 3px top rule, and nothing else, with one exception the build carries: on the fleet list, a fleet's name turns its tint on hover.

### Neutral
- **Review Ground** (`bg`): the page, the masthead, the tabs, the work pane, Needs items and the chat at rest, the composer box, inputs, keys, open menus, the sheet and the toast.
- **Card Grey** (`card`): thread card header strips, the supervisor's chat bubbles, and code in replies outside a bubble (inside one, code and tables sit on `bg`).
- **Panel Grey** (`panel`): the supervisor pane and the rail at board width.
- **Ink** (`fg`): body copy, headlines, the current tab and its underline, the author in a card header.
- **Muted Ink** (`muted`, and `neutral` at the same value): meta, times, Hand lines, the hint, crumbs, placeholders, stopped states and quiet counts.
- **Hairline** (`line`): every rule and decorative border (card edges, dividers), the context bar's track, and scrollbar thumbs.
- **Control Edge** (`line-strong`): the outline of what the operator operates, where the border is the affordance: select, input, textarea, the composer box, default buttons, the paperclip and the code copy button. It is also the edge of the supervisor's chat bubble, which sits on a fill only 1.04:1 (night 1.08:1) from the pane: `line` (1.17:1, night 1.25:1) cannot carry a bubble, `line-strong` does. `#80889a` by day (3.56:1 on `bg`, 3.20:1 on `card`, 3.32:1 on `panel`) and `#6b7482` at night (3.94:1, 3.50:1 and 3.77:1), so it meets the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11; `TestColoursMeetContrast` pins both.
- **Hover Wash** (`hover`): the hover fill of buttons, menu rows and the model menu's summary.
- **Terminal** (`screen`, `screen-fg`): a blocked screen's excerpt, dark in both themes.
- **Shadow** (`shadow`): the colour of the one lift shadow, under open menus and the toast.

### Signals
- **Fail Red** (`fail`, pale `fail-bg`): blocked screens, failed attempts and an interrupted supervisor in Needs; the failing pill (blocked, unreachable, exited, interrupted, failed); failing task icons; blocked and failed report headers; the auto-deny countdown; the context bar and its figure from 90%; error toasts and error pages; and the two controls that end a turn, Interrupt, and Stop on hover or armed.
- **Wait Amber** (`wait`, pale `wait-bg`): decisions, limited attempts and "no supervisor" items in Needs; a pending model switch; the context bar and its figure from 80%; a stale-server note; waiting task icons.
- **Pass Green** (`pass`, pale `pass-bg`): the ready pill, running and passing task icons, done report headers, the All clear icon, and done states.
- **Neutral** (`neutral`, pale `neutral-bg`): reports and quiet attempts in Needs; the starting, stopped and no-supervisor pills; idle task icons; counts at rest.

### Named Rules
**The Red Is Trouble Rule.** Fail red marks only what blocks or has broken: a blocked screen, a failure, an interrupted or unreachable supervisor, a context window at 90%, the auto-deny countdown and an error, plus the two controls that end a turn. Decisions, reports, links and ordinary states never take it.

**The Word And Shape Rule.** A state is never colour alone. The masthead pill carries an icon and a word, each Needs band head carries the severity word and each Needs row its icon, and each task row carries its state icon.

**The Tint Stays Put Rule.** The fleet tint colours the mark and the masthead's 3px top rule. It never fills a surface or colours copy.

## Typography

**Body Font:** the system sans (`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", "Noto Sans", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`)
**Label/Mono Font:** the system mono, held in `--mono` (`ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", monospace`)

**Character:** one plain sans for every word the operator reads, and a mono that appears only where the text is data: refs, keys, terminal lines, severity codes and figures that tick. There is no display face and no web font.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 22px, 30px, -0.01em): thread titles on the task and decision pages, balanced and clamped to two lines.
- **Headline** (600, 18px, 24px, -0.01em): the Needs and Tasks heads and the fleet list's head.
- **Title** (600, 15px, 22px): the headline of an open Needs item, the fleet name in the masthead and `###` headings in replies. A collapsed row sets it at 400 in Body type on one line with an ellipsis.
- **Copy** (400, 15px, 24px): the body of chat messages and thread cards; a chat message is held to a 72ch measure and thread cards fill their column; a bubble holds its tables and code blocks, which scroll inside it.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 20px): the page default, task rows, crumbs and the composer's text.
- **Heading small** (600, 14px, 20px): `h3` heads on thread pages and `####` headings in replies; report titles in a report header use 14px at 600.
- **Nav** (600, 14px, 20px): the tabs, the model menu's summary and the model name in the pane head.
- **Control** (400, 14px, 20px): buttons, with the primary at 600. Keys in a blocked screen take the same size in mono.
- **Badge** (600, 12px, 18px, tabular): every badge: the status pills, the Needs counts, the task groups' counts, deliverable links, report states and the countdown.
- **Label** (600, 12px, 18px): the task groups' heads and labels in menus.
- **Meta** (400, 12px, 18px, tabular): times, meta, message head lines, card header strips, Hand lines, the working line, hints and notes.
- **Mono** (400, 12px, 18px, tabular): refs, the fleet id, `kbd` keycaps, the context figure and the countdown (at 600).
- **Severity code** (600, 12px, 18px, 0.03em, uppercase mono): the severity word on the task page's waiting rows. A Needs band head sets the same word in Label type, in sentence case (Blocked, Failed, Limited, No supervisor, Stalled, Interrupted, Decision, Report, Quiet, Long turn).
- **Code block** (400, 12px, 18px, mono): terminal excerpts and preformatted blocks.

### Named Rules
**The Five Sizes Rule.** Every pixel font size is 22, 18, 15, 14 or 12px. `TestTypeHasFiveSteps` fails on any other, with one pinned exception: inputs rise to 16px on coarse pointers so a phone never zooms. Inline code in replies sets at 12px.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Figures that update in place are set in tabular numerals so the row never shifts: the `.live` class (the context figure, the countdown), every `time`, counts, meta and refs.

## Layout

- **Rail (900px and wider):** a column fixed to the left edge, 56px wide and the full height, on `panel` with a hairline on its right. The page sits to its right: the body takes 56px of left padding, so the masthead, the grid and every thread page start after it, and the pane clamp below is a percentage of the space that is left. Its top cell is 48px high, the height of the masthead, with the label button and a hairline under it. The rail has no shadow and stays 56px at every window width (the label button widens it on request): at 900px the work pane is 444px and the chat 400px.

The tab comes from the URL hash, and an id inside a panel (such as `#msg-i3`) opens the tab that holds it; with no hash it opens on Needs when anything waits and on Chat otherwise. The board never switches tabs for the operator. Without JavaScript all three panels show and the tabs are anchors. Nothing resizes below 900px.
- **Fleet page at 900px and wider:** a grid of the work pane (`minmax(0,1fr)`) and the supervisor pane (`--pane`) with no gap. The supervisor pane is a column on `panel` with a hairline on its left: the pane head, the timeline, then the composer. The tabs are hidden, the Needs head and the Tasks head show, and the masthead's inner row spans the page with 24px of padding.

The task page widens its column to 1120px, and the masthead aligns to its 1088px content.

### Named Rules
**The Two Panes At Board Width Rule.** The work pane and the supervisor pane sit side by side from 900px (`TestNeedsAndTasksSitBesideTheChatAtBoardWidth`); below it the three tabs stay and the separator is absent (`TestTabsBelowBoardWidth`).

**The Chat Width Clamp Rule.** The chat's width is the custom property `--chat`, and only `--pane` turns it into a grid track, clamped to 360px at least and everything but 440px at most. `app.js` sets `--chat` through the CSSOM, which the CSP allows, and never lays the pane out itself (`TestTheChatPaneResizesInsideTheClamp`).

**The Slim Rail Rule.** Navigation is a 56px rail of icons from 900px and a sheet below it; the work pane stays the widest thing on the page, so there is no text sidebar, and the chat is dragged to size with no fold or widen mode (`TestThePhoneSheetHoldsTheRailsLinks`, `TestTheRailIsSixTabStopsBeforeTheMasthead`).

**The Proven Fleets Rule.** The rail lists the current fleet and, beside it, only the fleets whose login the browser sends and that fleet accepts. A fleet the browser holds no valid token for never has its name in the page, and no fleet's Needs count shows in another's rail. A network board lists only its own fleet (`TestTheRailListsOnlyTheFleetsTheViewerCanProve`, `TestTheCurrentFleetIsAlwaysListed`, `TestANetworkBoardListsOnlyItsOwnFleet`, `TestTheRailListsOnlyFleetsTheBrowserHoldsALiveTokenFor`).

**The Five Regions Rule.** The fleet page updates live through exactly five regions, `status`, `console`, `timeline`, `queue` and `tasks`, and the composer form sits outside every region so a live update never takes the operator's draft. The console region sits in the supervisor pane's head, and the task search form sits in the Tasks head outside the tasks region, for the same reason.

**The Things In New Tabs Rule.** From the Board, things open in a new tab and places navigate in place; outside links always open in a new tab. A thing is a task, attempt, report, decision, deliverable, image or outside page; a place is a fleet, the Board, Tasks, Conversation, a crumb, a pager step or an anchor. The fleet page's task rows, Needs links and refs in replies and Hand lines carry `target="_blank" rel="noopener"` (`TestAwayOpensLiveRefsInANewTab`); the Tasks page, the conversation page and the thread pages browse refs in place (`TestTheTasksPageBrowsesInPlace`, `TestTheLogAndThreadPagesKeepRefsInPlace`). Middle-click and Ctrl-click work everywhere.

## Elevation & Depth

The Board is flat. Depth comes from tone and hairlines: cards and header strips on `card` or a pale signal fill, borders in `line`, and a night card one step above the night ground. Only two things float, the open menus and the toast, and they share one soft lift. The keys of a blocked screen carry a 1px keycap edge, and `kbd` keycaps a 2px bottom border instead of a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px var(--shadow)`): the open model menu, the overflow menu and the toast.
- **Focus frame** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px var(--accent)`): with an accent border, the composer box's 2px frame while its text box has focus. A focus state, not depth.
- **Keycap edge** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 var(--line)`): the bottom edge of each key under a blocked screen.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Board Rule.** Resting surfaces carry a hairline and no shadow. `TestTheOnCallPalette` fails on any `box-shadow` outside the composer box, the menus, the toast, the keys and `kbd`.

On a phone the selects come first and the button spans the row. With no routing policy the details start open. ### Composer
One box on the ground with a 1px control-edge border and 10px corners, padded 4px by 4px by 8px, set in the pane's 760px column and pinned under the timeline. It is 90px high at rest (102px on touch), and 94px while the hint wraps to a second line. Its focus frame is an accent border plus a 1px accent ring.
- **Draft:** the text survives navigation, new tabs, reloads and restarts. It is kept in `localStorage` per fleet (`hand.draft.<fleet id>`, text and time), written 300ms after the last keystroke and when the page is hidden or unloads, restored when the box is empty and never over text already there, and cleared by a successful send or by emptying the box by hand. A failed send keeps it. A removal made in another tab clears this box only when it holds exactly the text that was removed. Another tab's draft reaches this box only when the box is empty or still holds what that tab last sent it; a box with its own unsent text keeps it, and the last tab to save wins in storage. Text over 200,000 characters stays in the box and is not stored; a draft older than 30 days is dropped on load. A decision's note is kept the same way per decision (`hand.draft.<fleet id>.<decision id>`) and cleared by its answer. The draft is local to this browser, stored as plain text and never sent anywhere; its 30 days count from the last save, and attachments are not kept. Text typed while a send is in flight stays in the box and in storage. With storage blocked or full the box works without it (`TestTheDraftIsKeptInLocalStorageByFleet`).
- **Draft note:** a muted 12px "Draft saved 14:32", or "Draft restored" after a restore, set in the composer's top border at the right edge (`.draft-note`, absolute, so the box never grows). It is empty, and so hidden, when nothing is stored, and is not a live region (`TestTheComposerShowsADraftNote`).
- **Text box:** at least 44px high, borderless and transparent, growing with its text up to 40dvh (30dvh on short screens and without JavaScript).
- **Foot row:** the hint in muted meta, which wraps rather than truncates, then the send group at the right edge. The hint says only why a message would wait, as in "s1 is starting; your message waits." (also a stop with its reason, no supervisor, a Luvus restart, a blocked screen and a switch after this turn). While the supervisor is ready or working it is empty and the row is the send group's height (`TestTheComposerHintFollowsTheState`).
  - **Send group:** the paperclip "Attach files" button (a square of `--control`), the send key hint ("Ctrl Enter", or "⌘ Enter" on Apple devices, hidden on coarse pointers and while the chat pane is under 520px wide) and the primary Send.
  - **Hint:** while the supervisor is blocked, it links to Needs. While files upload, it reads "Attaching n file(s)…". While a Start or Resume form is in the pane head, Send turns from primary to a default button.
  - **Attaching:** paste, a drop on the composer and the paperclip each attach any file up to 10 MiB; the picker has no type filter. During a drag, the composer shows an accent dashed outline.

### Separator
A drag strip on the supervisor pane's left edge, 10px wide (24px on coarse pointers) and made by `app.js` as `div.split`, so a page without JavaScript carries no dead control. It is hidden below 900px. It is a `role="separator"` with `aria-orientation="vertical"`, `aria-controls="chat"` and `aria-label="Chat width"`, focusable, with `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and `aria-valuenow` in chat pixels and an `aria-valuetext` that reads "547 pixels".
- **At rest:** only the pane's hairline. On hover, focus or drag, a 3px accent line and a 36px grip show; on touch the grip always shows.
- **Pointer:** pointer events with capture, so a drag that leaves the window keeps working. The chat width follows the pointer one to one, between the limits; a click that moves nothing stores nothing.
- **Keys:** Left and Right move the separator by 16px (64px with Shift), so Left widens the chat; Home sets the narrowest chat, End the widest, and Enter resets. A double-click resets too.
- **Remembered:** one width for the whole board in `localStorage` under `hand.chat`, removed on reset. Storage errors are caught, so with storage blocked the separator works for the session; a stored value that is not a positive number is ignored.
- **No fold and no widen mode:** the chat is dragged to size, never folded or maximised.

### Menus
The model menu and the overflow menu are `details` elements whose body opens below the summary (the overflow menu's at the right edge) on the ground, with a hairline border, 10px corners, the lift shadow and 12px padding. The model menu offers a profile select (each profile naming what it runs), model and effort lists for the current harness, and "Switch now" or "Switch after this turn", then one switch form per other harness. ### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded corners (6px).
- **Height:** every button, input, select, the paperclip and the model menu's summary is `--control` high (32px; 44px on coarse pointers), and a key is at least that wide. The text of a button without an icon is trimmed to its cap height (`text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic`) and the box padded by `(--control - 1cap) / 2`, so capitals sit on the box's centre whatever the font; browsers without `text-box` fall back to centring the line. The trim is a default, `button:where(:not(:has(.icon)))` and Stop, with the specificity of a plain `button` and set after it, so a component's own box overrides it.
- **Default:** ground fill, control-edge border, ink text in Control type, padded 0 by 12px; hover takes the hover wash.
- **Primary:** Ink with `on-primary` text at 600 and no visible border, one per form; hover mixes 14% of the ground into it.
- **Disabled and busy:** disabled buttons fade to 55%; a form in flight fades its buttons to 60% with a progress cursor.
- **Focus:** a 2px accent outline, 2px out, with 4px corners, on every focusable element.

### Refs and badges
- **Ref:** plain mono text at 12px in the surrounding colour; a linked ref is accent and underlines on hover, or with a dotted underline inside a reply. Inside slug lines and Hand lines a ref takes the line's colour.
- **Badge:** the one `.badge`, a pill at least `--badge` (20px) wide and high, in Badge type, with a transparent 1px border and 0 by 8px padding. The text is trimmed to its cap height and the box padded by `(height - 1cap) / 2 - 1px`, so capitals sit within 0.6px of the centre. The status pill and the masthead's Needs count are `--badge` plus 4px (24px), set through `--size` on the badge. Its tone comes from one set of rules keyed on the attribute the markup already carries, and a badge with none is neutral: `data-tone` (`fail`, `wait`, `pass`, `link`, `accent`), `data-state` (the pills: `failing` red, `waiting` amber, `ready`, `running` and `passing` green, `working` accent), `data-status` (a report's `stuck`, `progress` and `done`) and `data-worst` (a Needs count: red for blocked, worker, failure and resume; amber for decision, nosup, limited and stalled). The badges are the status pills, the Needs counts (masthead, tab and head), the task groups' counts, the severity word on the task page's waiting rows and the error page, the auto-deny countdown, a report's state, the deliverable link (`PR`, `MR` or `link`, outlined in a hairline, opening in a new tab) and the type on a sent file card. In a Needs header the severity word drops its fill and padding.
- **Corner mark:** an outside link (a deliverable chip, or a link in a reply, plan, report or question) ends in a 5px corner drawn with two 1.5px borders, no glyph and no image, at 70% of the text colour (`TestOutsideLinksCarryACornerMark`). A ref carries no mark, since every ref on the Board opens a tab.
- **Deliverable file:** plain muted mono text with no box (its basename cut with an ellipsis, the full path in its title).
- **Choice list:** how a decision is answered, one component in a Needs item and on the decision page. The question's lines that read `N. Label — consequence (recommended)` leave the question (a later numbered list that reuses a key, such as steps after the options, stays in it) and become a bordered list with 10px corners and hairlines between its rows (`TestTheChoiceListIsOneComponentInNeedsAndOnThePage`). A row is a `label` around a visually hidden radio, 48px high at least (52px on coarse pointers; `.option+.option` takes 1px less padding so the hairline never adds to it), padded 12px by 14px, with a 24px numbered circle, the label in Body type at 600 over the consequence in muted Body type, and the green `Recommended` badge at the end of the label's line (it wraps under a label that leaves it no room). A label over 90 characters sits at 400. The circle's number is Badge type in mono and cap-centred (`text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic`, padded by `(24px - 2px - 1cap) / 2`); a picked row fills the circle and the row with the accent and the accent wash, and the focused row draws the 2px accent focus ring inside its edge. Below the list sit the note (a one-line textarea that grows with its text, `--control` high, placeholder "Add a note (optional)") and one primary Send answer; the keys are on the rows' circles, so no keys hint repeats them. A row whose label starts with "Other" makes the note required ("Write your answer") and a key or click on it moves focus to the note, and the server refuses such a pick without one. Send needs a pick or a note: "Pick an option or write an answer." appears under the button. The server sends `2. Track 0.14.x`, a blank line and the note. A refresh of the Needs region keeps the picked row and the note. Without option lines the same form is a plain required answer box. A failed send writes its message under Send (a red `role=alert` line) and keeps the form; a decision answered elsewhere swaps the form for the answered state with "d5 is already answered.", and the status badge follows.
- **Answered state:** the options as muted rows with the chosen one marked by the filled circle, the accent wash and a `Your answer` badge (the recommended row keeps its badge), then the operator's note as a card, or a line "Answered by operator (board) · time" when there is none. An answer that names no option (a free text, or a number the question does not list) shows only as the card.
- **Keycap:** `kbd` is `--badge` high with a 2px bottom edge.

### Rows
One `.row` serves the attempt and task rows, the waiting rows and decision rows on the task page, and the fleet list. It is a flex line at least 40px high with 4px of padding and a hairline under it, with five named slots in this order:
- **lead:** the state icon or the fleet's mark (`.icon`, `.mark`);
- **ref:** the ref or the slug (`.ref`, `.slug`);
- **body:** the title (`.check-title`, `.check-body`, `.need-title`, `.fleet-name`), which takes the free width and truncates;
- **meta:** the muted meta text (`.meta`), shrinking before the body;
- **tail:** everything else, in markup order: a time, a form, a link or an answer.

A slot that is missing leaves no gap. `.wrap` lets the waiting and decision rows wrap their body onto its own line on a narrow screen, and a decision's answer takes a line of its own. Task rows keep their 48px height and the whole row as the link; fleet rows are 48px.

### Inputs
Inputs and selects are `--control` high on the ground with a control-edge border, 6px corners and 0 by 10px padding; text boxes pad 8px by 10px. Focus draws a 2px accent outline inset by 1px and hides the border. Placeholders are muted. A select and its options set their own ground and ink (`bg`, `fg`), and `:root` declares `color-scheme: light dark`, so the native list opens in the theme's colours (`TestSelectsAndTheirOptionsKeepTheThemeColours`).

### Code copy button
Every code block in rendered markdown (replies, plans, reports, decision text, task page cards) gets a copy button from `app.js`; the renderer's markup is unchanged and without script the block reads as before.
- **Structure:** `app.js` wraps each `.md pre` in `div.code` (position relative) and appends `button.copy`, type button, holding three 16px icons of which one shows at a time: copy, a tick, a cross. It runs on load and after every region replacement, so it is idempotent and covers messages that arrive later. A focused button keeps focus across a refresh.
- **Place and size:** the button is `--control` square (32px; 44px on coarse pointers) in the block's top right corner, 4px in, on the block's own surface with a `line-strong` edge and 6px corners. The wrapper holds it, so it stays put while a long line scrolls under it; the block keeps a right padding of `--control` plus 12px so the end of a line clears it, and a minimum height that holds the button. A scrolled line passes under the button.
- **When it shows:** on hover or focus within the block at desk, always on coarse pointers, and whenever the button is focused or has a state. It takes the 2px accent focus ring.
- **Copy:** the `pre`'s text exactly, through `navigator.clipboard.writeText`, or a hidden `textarea` and `execCommand("copy")` where the clipboard API is absent or refuses. For 1.5s the icon is a green tick and the name reads "Copied", or a red cross and "Copy failed"; then it returns to "Copy code". The state is spoken through one visually hidden `role=status` region. It never opens an alert.
- **Colour:** the icon is `muted` on the block's surface (6.15:1 on `bg`, 5.52:1 on `card`, night 7.23:1 and 6.43:1); the edge is `line-strong` (3.2:1 or better on both surfaces). With forced colours the icon and edge take `ButtonText`.
- **Motion:** the fade-in is .15s and stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Scroll to latest button
A round button above the composer that returns the operator to the newest message after they scrolled up. `app.js` adds it, so without script the page is unchanged; it is not on the conversation page, a short page of at most 50 entries with no composer to sit above.
- **Structure:** a `button.latest` (type button) holding the down arrow and a `.badge` for the count, set as the first child of the composer box, before its form, so it follows the timeline's content and precedes the text box in the tab order; on a read-only board it sits in the same place on the read-only note. A visually hidden `role=status` region beside it speaks the count. It lives outside the five regions, so a live update never replaces it or loses its count.

### Named Rules
**The Copy Stays With The Code Rule.** A code block is copied from its own button, added by `app.js` and never by the renderer, which also serves the CLI. The button copies the block's text byte for byte, says what happened in words and in the live region, and is reachable by keyboard.

**The Bands Rule.** Needs groups its open items under one head per severity word, most severe first, and the head carries the word and its tone, so a row repeats neither. A collapsed row is one calm line and only the open item is a card.

**The One Choice List Rule.** A decision is answered from one component, the choice list, in a Needs item and on the decision page, and the answered state is the same list at rest. The question never repeats its option lines, and the server joins the pick and the note into one answer.

**The One Badge And One Row Rule.** Every pill, count and tag is the one `.badge`, and every line of the task, attempt, event, decision and fleet lists is the one `.row`. A new component sets a slot or a tone on them; it never draws a third shape.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from the custom properties in `board.css` (`var(--fail)`, `var(--accent-bg)`), and give any new token both a light and a dark value that keeps 4.5:1 for text.
- **Do** order anything that needs the operator by the severity ramp in `internal/board/queue.go`, group it under one head per severity word, and fill the open item's header with its tone's pale fill.
- **Do** give every state a word and an icon shape as well as its colour.
- **Do** keep font sizes to 22, 18, 15, 14 and 12px, and set figures that update in place in tabular numerals.
- **Do** leave a trace for every action: a receipt, a Hand line in Chat, and a link back to the item.
- **Do** keep one primary button per form, and keep Stop behind a second press.
- **Do** keep all style in `board.css` and all script in `app.js`: no inline script, no inline style and no `style` attribute. `app.js` may set the `--chat` custom property and the rail tip's `top` through the CSSOM, which the CSP allows.
- **Do** stop every animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`: the working pulse, the feed, the arrival wash, the open and close transition, the copy button's fade and the scroll to latest button's fade.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use fail red for decisions, reports, links or ordinary states.
- **Don't** put a shadow on a resting surface; only the open menus and the toast lift.
- **Don't** colour copy or fill a surface with the fleet tint.
- **Don't** bring back the newsroom words (WIRE, FLASH, BULLETIN, URGENT, ROUTINE, SERVICE, dispatch, news budget, tray) or the canary, carbon, ink band, `h-*` and `pencil` tokens.
- **Don't** load web fonts or images from the stylesheet.
- **Don't** draw an icon as a text glyph or pull in an icon library; add it to `icons.html` in the 16px, 1.5-stroke family.
- **Don't** show both panes below 900px or resize anything there, and don't switch tabs for the operator.
- **Don't** add a text sidebar or a third column beside the two panes. Navigation is the 56px icon rail, its names showing on hover, on focus or on request, and the work pane stays the widest thing on the page. Don't make a green-on-black terminal look beyond the screen excerpt.
- **Don't** list a fleet in the rail, or show its name or count anywhere in a page, unless the browser holds a valid token for it.
