# Security

Report a vulnerability through the repository's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/atqamz/hand/security/advisories/new), never in a public issue. Include:

- all of `hand version`, and your OS and architecture;
- what an attacker can do, and what they need first, such as a local account or a page open in your browser;
- the exact steps that show it;
- redacted output: no tokens, login links, user names or paths.

Fixes are best effort, with no timeline, and land in a new release that `hand update` installs.
